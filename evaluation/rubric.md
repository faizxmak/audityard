# Public scoring summary

This summary describes the supplied 100-point rubric. The complete evaluator guide and answer key are retained locally and are excluded from this public repository.

| Category | Points | What is assessed |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge-case conclusions | 48 | Two points for each of 24 materially correct conclusions, one for a partial conclusion without a false definitive allegation, zero for an incorrect conclusion |
| Evidence discipline | 20 | Eighteen points across six selected challenge cases for distinguishing supported facts, uncertainty, and appropriate follow-up, plus two for source integrity |
| Quantification | 15 | Five each for cash reconciliation, individual monetary findings, and separation of recoverable amounts, affected payments, uncertain exposure, and unpaid obligations |
| Coverage and traceability | 10 | Five for all 184 orders and five for evidence and policy citations that support the claims |
| Follow-up quality | 7 | Six targeted evidence requests and appropriate prioritization of confirmed nonperformance and duplicate recovery |

Definitive unsupported allegations in the designated uncertainty cases incur an additional five-point deduction each, capped at 20 points, and fail the uncertainty gate. The final score is bounded between zero and 100. The suggested pass criterion is at least 85 points with the uncertainty gate passed.

Equivalent wording is accepted. The 160 ordinary cases add reconciliation volume; they do not count as 160 independent difficult reasoning tests. Confidence should distinguish certainty that a record is missing from certainty about whether the underlying activity occurred.

The rubric requires evaluator materials to be inaccessible to tested agents. The recorded run excluded them from its input handoff but did not enforce filesystem isolation. Its score is therefore reported with that limitation.

The public repository supports checking the reported arithmetic and inspecting the assessment. It does not contain everything needed for a fully independent answer-key rescore. See [assessment.md](assessment.md) for deductions and [README.md](README.md) for the test conditions.
